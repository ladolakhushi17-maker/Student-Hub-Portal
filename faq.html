Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about StudentHub.

1. What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is an online portal that helps students access academic information, events, notices, and their profiles.

2. How do I create an account?

Go to the Register page and fill in the required details to create your account.

3. How do I log in?

Open the Login page and enter your registered email address and password.

4. What can I do on the Dashboard?

You can view your profile, academic information, events, and other important updates.

5. Who can use StudentHub?

Students, teachers, and administrators can use the StudentHub portal according to their roles.
